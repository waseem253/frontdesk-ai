AI Voice Receptionist & Booking — Sequence WHAT HAPPENS WHEN

Step by step over time · one inbound call, then one outbound retry chain

Inbound: a customer calls the shop

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant Cu as Customer
  participant Tw as Twilio
  participant AI as AI Voice Agent
  participant SA as Service-area check
  participant SMS as Twilio SMS
  participant Ch as Team group-chat
  participant Sh as Google Sheets
  Cu->>Tw: dials your number
  Tw->>AI: route the call
  AI->>Cu: greeting (your script, your voice)
  loop natural conversation
    AI->>Cu: asks for name / phone / appliance / problem / window / access
    Cu->>AI: answers
  end
  AI->>SA: check ZIP
  alt out of service area
    AI->>Cu: polite "we don't service that area" + offers escalation
    AI->>Ch: alert admin (out-of-area lead)
  else in service area
    AI->>Cu: reads $89 diagnostic-fee script verbatim
    AI->>Cu: offers windows (9–12 · 10–12 · 12–2 · 2–4 · 4–6)
    Cu->>AI: picks a window
    AI->>SMS: send confirmation (company, window, address, fee, 30-min heads-up)
    SMS->>Cu: SMS arrives
    AI->>Ch: post full booking + call summary
    AI->>Sh: append row (admin backup)
    AI->>Cu: confirms verbally + ends call
  end
  Note over AI,Ch: any of the 7 escalation triggers fires → transfer or notify admin instead of guessing

Outbound: a new lead arrives from your lead source

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant LP as Lead source
  participant Au as Automation
  participant AI as AI Outbound Caller
  participant Le as Lead (the prospect)
  participant Ch as Team group-chat
  participant Sh as Google Sheets
  LP->>Au: new lead webhook (within seconds)
  Au->>AI: trigger call within minutes
  AI->>Le: places call · Attempt 1
  alt answered
    AI->>Le: qualifies + books through the same inbound flow
    AI->>Ch: posts full booking + summary
    AI->>Sh: append row (booked)
  else no answer
    AI->>Ch: alert — Attempt 1 · no-answer · short summary
    Au->>AI: wait per retry schedule
    AI->>Le: Attempt 2 (next day window)
    AI->>Ch: alert — Attempt 2 · outcome + summary
    Au->>AI: wait per retry schedule
    AI->>Le: Attempt 3 (~3 days total)
    AI->>Ch: alert — Attempt 3 · outcome + summary
    alt still no answer
      Au->>Sh: mark closed
      Au->>Ch: final alert — lead closed
    end
  end
  Note over Au,Le: retry cadence and number of attempts are configurable to your preference
Internal alerts every attempt — outcome (answered / no-answer / voicemail) + short call summary, never silent Same booking core — an answered outbound call books exactly the same way as an inbound call You set the cadence — number of retries and spacing across the days are configurable
